Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Morrow & Made (morrowandmade.com) is operated by an individual seller based in Maryland. This page explains what information is collected when you visit our site, sign up for our free guide, or buy from us, and who collects it.

Our website

morrowandmade.com is a simple static page hosted on GitHub Pages.

  • We use no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools, and our site has no forms. We don't collect anything from you on morrowandmade.com itself.
  • GitHub, our host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub handles that under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Free guide sign-up

You sign up for our free guide, Just Engaged: Your First 30 Days, on a page run by systeme.io, our email service. The page is linked from our site.

  • What we collect: your email address, the date you signed up, and that you asked for the free guide.
  • How we use it: to send you the guide, then our short wedding-planning emails, which can mention our products.
  • Who stores it: systeme.io stores the list and sends the emails for us. It may record whether emails are opened or links are clicked. systeme.io has its own privacy policy and may use its own cookies on its pages.
  • Unsubscribing: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to hello@morrowandmade.com.
  • How long we keep it: until you ask us to delete it. If you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you.

When you buy

When you click to buy, you go to a checkout run by Payhip. Card payments are processed by Stripe through Payhip.

  • Payhip collects your email address and name to deliver your download and send your receipt. It may also collect billing details needed for tax. Payhip shares your order details with us as the seller, such as your name, email, what you bought, and the price.
  • Payhip may add buyers to our email list. Buyers in the EU and UK are asked first. Anyone can unsubscribe with the link in any marketing email, or by writing to hello@morrowandmade.com.
  • Make.com (a free automation service) receives each order from Payhip and passes the buyer's email address and what they bought to systeme.io, so we can send a thank-you email with our free guide and tailor our emails to what you bought. Make has its own privacy policy on make.com.
  • Stripe processes your card. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Payhip and Stripe each have their own privacy policies and may use their own cookies on their pages:
    • https://payhip.com/privacy
    • https://stripe.com/privacy

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver and support your purchase
  • Handle refunds
  • Keep the business records the law requires
  • Send Morrow & Made emails to people who signed up for our free guide and to buyers on our list, as described above

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't share your email address with anyone except the services named on this page, which handle it for us.

Email to us

If you email hello@morrowandmade.com, we receive your email address and whatever is in your message. We use it only to reply to you. Please don't send passwords, card details, or your guest list.

Your files stay yours

Our spreadsheets run in your own Google or Microsoft account, or on your own computer. We can't see anything you type into them.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep order records for as long as we need them for tax and accounting. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your other information by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your local privacy law. We'll honor those rights.

Visitors in the UK or EU: Morrow & Made decides how your data is used (contact: hello@morrowandmade.com). We send you marketing emails only if you signed up for our free guide or agreed at checkout. You can object or unsubscribe at any time, free of charge, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Do Not Track: our site doesn't track you across other websites, so there's nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
