Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Morrow & Made (morrowandmade.com) is operated by an individual seller based in Maryland. This page explains what information is collected when you visit our site, ask to see our price, or buy Wedding Planning OS, and who collects it.

Our website

morrowandmade.com is a simple static page hosted on GitHub Pages.

  • We use no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools. The only thing we collect on our site is the email address you enter to see our price (see "Email sign-up" below).
  • Your browser remembers that you've already seen the price, so we don't ask for your email twice. This is a small yes/no note saved on your device, not your email address. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
  • GitHub, our host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub handles that under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Email sign-up

When you click to buy, we ask for your email address before showing the price.

  • What we collect: your email address and the date you signed up.
  • How we use it: to show you the price and to send you Morrow & Made emails: wedding-planning tips, product news and occasional offers. The form says this before you sign up.
  • Who stores it: our email service, Kit (kit.com), stores the list and sends the emails for us. Kit may record whether emails are opened or links are clicked. Kit's privacy policy: https://kit.com/privacy
  • Unsubscribing: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to morrowandmade@yahoo.com. We act on requests within 10 business days.
  • How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

When you buy

When you continue to checkout after seeing the price, you go to a checkout run by Payhip. Card payments are processed by Stripe through Payhip.

  • Payhip collects your email address and name to deliver your download and send your receipt. It may also collect billing details needed for tax. Payhip shares your order details with us as the seller, such as your name, email, what you bought, and the price.
  • Payhip may add buyers to our email list. Buyers in the EU and UK are asked first. Everyone else can unsubscribe from any marketing email.
  • Stripe processes your card. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Payhip and Stripe each have their own privacy policies and may use their own cookies on their pages:
    • https://payhip.com/privacy
    • https://stripe.com/privacy

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver and support your purchase
  • Handle refunds
  • Keep the business records the law requires
  • Send Morrow & Made emails to people on our list, as described above

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't share your email address with anyone except the services named on this page, which handle it for us.

Email to us

If you email morrowandmade@yahoo.com, we receive your email address and whatever is in your message. We use it only to reply to you. Please don't send passwords, card details, or your guest list.

Your files stay yours

The workbook runs in your own Google or Microsoft account, or on your own computer. We can't see anything you type into it.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep order records for as long as we need them for tax and accounting. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your other information by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your local privacy law. We'll honor those rights.

Visitors in the UK or EU: Morrow & Made decides how your data is used (contact: morrowandmade@yahoo.com). We email you because you asked to see our price and were told about our emails when you signed up, or because you agreed at checkout. You can object or unsubscribe at any time, free of charge, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Do Not Track: our site doesn't track you across other websites, so there's nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
