Before you buy

A few useful things to know.

Which spreadsheet apps does it work in?

Google Sheets and Excel for the web have been tested. In Excel for the web, move between sections with the sheet tabs at the bottom; the Start Here shortcut links don't work there. Recent desktop Excel should open the files, but we haven't manually tested it yet. Excel 2016 and Apple Numbers aren't supported.

Does it work on my phone?

It's designed for planning on a laptop or desktop. You can view it in the Google Sheets or Excel mobile apps, but editing on a small screen is not the intended experience.

Does anything happen automatically?

Totals, statuses and warnings update from the information you enter. You record payments yourself. The workbook doesn't sync with your bank or wedding website, import RSVPs, or send email or push reminders.

Can we plan together?

Yes. Use the sharing settings in Google Sheets or Excel to invite your partner or family. Only share with people you want to see your planning details.

Can I use a currency other than US dollars?

Yes. Change the currency label on the Start Here tab and enter every amount in that currency. The workbook doesn't convert exchange rates.

How do I get my files?

Right after checkout you'll get a download link on screen and by email. The download is a ZIP file with the Excel and Google Sheets versions and the setup guide.

Is this a planning service?

No. It's a digital organizing tool. Wedding planning services, calls, coaching and custom spreadsheet work aren't included. Your vendor contracts always decide what you actually owe.
