Refund Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Morrow & Made sells digital downloads: Wedding Planning OS, the Seating & Tables Planner, the DIY Day-Of Coordinator Kit, the Vendor Hiring & Contract Kit, and bundles of these. Morrow & Made is operated by an individual seller based in Maryland. Checkout and delivery are handled by Payhip, and card payments are processed by Stripe through Payhip.

This policy applies the same way to every product we sell, including bundles.

What you're buying

A one-time purchase of digital files: spreadsheets that open in Excel and Google Sheets, and in some products printable PDF guides. Each product page lists exactly what's included. Payhip delivers your download right after payment. There's nothing to ship, and there's no subscription.

When we'll refund you

Email hello@morrowandmade.com within 7 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full if either of these applies:

  1. The files don't open or don't work as described in a supported app, and we can't fix the problem for you. Supported apps are:

    • Google Sheets
    • Excel for the web
    • Recent desktop Excel (Microsoft 365, or Excel 2019 or later)
    • For PDF guides, an up-to-date PDF reader

    Please tell us which product and app you used and what went wrong. Most access problems can be fixed quickly, so we'll try that first.

  2. You bought it twice by accident. We'll refund the duplicate order.

When we generally can't refund

Once the files have been downloaded, a digital purchase can't be returned. That means we generally don't refund for:

  • Changing your mind, or finding you'd rather plan in a different way
  • Using an app we don't support. For Wedding Planning OS, that includes Excel 2016 and Apple Numbers, which its product page lists before you buy.
  • Features a product doesn't claim to have. For example, Wedding Planning OS has no bank sync, no automatic email or phone reminders, no currency conversion, and no automatic link to a wedding website.
  • Problems caused by editing or deleting a spreadsheet's formulas. We're still happy to help you get back to a clean copy.

How refunds work

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Payhip and Stripe.
  • How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or card issuer.
  • If you're refunded, please delete your copies of the files.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you.
